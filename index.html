The Three-Layer AI Visibility Gap

Not Found

AI models can't recommend what they can't see. If your content isn't structured for AI crawlers, you're invisible to the fastest-growing search channel.

Not Cited

Even when AI finds your content, it may not reference you. Without clear authority signals and structured claims, you're data without attribution.

Not Chosen

Being found and cited isn't enough. AI chooses the clearest, most trustworthy answer. If that's not you, a competitor gets the recommendation.

The Amplifier Effect

AI lowers the build barrier, meaning more competitors in every market. When anyone can build, discoverability becomes the number-one determinant of who wins.
