### 4.6 Proof Points Summary

- **Immediate clarity delivery.** A confusing, abstract problem ("am I visible to AI?") becomes three specific, fixable things within seconds.

#### Tier 2: Risk Reversal
- **3 Gaps Guarantee.** Every scan reveals at least three actionable gaps. If it does not, the scan did not do its job.
- **Free entry point.** Free scan with no account required. Value delivered before any transaction.

- **Founder uses the tools on his own sites.** Jamie runs AI Search Mastery on FreecalcHub and aisearchmastery.com. Practitioner first, vendor second.

### 4.7 What We Never Say

These guardrails protect the brand voice and the trust it is built on. As messaging scales beyond Jamie, these are the lines that do not get crossed.

#### Words and Phrases We Do Not Use

| Never Say | Why | Say Instead |
|-----------|-----|-------------|
| "Guaranteed rankings" / "Guaranteed results" | We can't control AI model behavior. No one can. | "Measurable improvement" / "Track your progress" |
| "Dominate" / "Crush" / "Destroy the competition" | Aggressive, zero-sum language contradicts our values. | "Show up" / "Be discoverable" / "Be recommended" |
| "Secret" / "Proprietary algorithm" / "Our secret sauce" | Our methodology is published. Implying secrecy contradicts our core differentiator. | "Open methodology" / "Published framework" |
| "Revolutionary" / "Game-changing" / "Disrupting" | Hyperbole that signals insecurity. The product should speak for itself. | Describe the specific thing that is different. |
| "Unleash" / "Supercharge" / "Turbocharge" / "10x" | Marketing cliches that erode trust with our audience. | Describe the actual outcome in specific terms. |
| "Don't miss out" / "Act now" / "Limited time" | Manufactured urgency. We do not do it. | "Here when you're ready" / "Start with the free scan" |
| "Simple" / "Easy" / "Just" | Dismisses the real complexity our customers face. | "Clear" / "Specific" / "Step-by-step" |
| "We're the best" / "Industry-leading" / "#1" | Unsubstantiated superlatives. We are early-stage. | State the specific thing we do that no one else does. |
| "AI-powered" (about our own tools) | Vague and overused. Be specific about what the technology actually does. | Name the specific capability. |
| "You need this" / "You can't afford not to" | Presumptuous. We present information and let people decide. | "Here's what we found" / "Here's what it means" |

#### Tones We Do Not Strike

- **Vendor authority.** We are not the expert lecturing from above. We are a practitioner sharing what we have built and learned.
- **False confidence.** AI search is evolving fast. We do not pretend to have all the answers. When we do not know something, we say so.
- **Condescension toward "doing nothing."** Our real competitor is inaction, but we respect it. "Doing nothing" is often a rational response to uncertainty. We reduce the uncertainty; we do not shame the hesitation.
- **Scarcity theater.** No countdown timers. No "only 3 spots left." No artificial urgency of any kind.
- **Competitor bashing.** We name what we do differently. We do not attack other tools or companies.

#### Claims We Do Not Make

- **We do not claim to know how specific AI models weight their recommendations internally.** Our framework is based on published research, observable behavior, and practitioner testing. It is our best-evidence model, not ground truth from inside OpenAI.
- **We do not promise specific revenue outcomes.** The 134x value gap is a math illustration, not a guarantee.
- **We do not claim AI search has "replaced" traditional search.** It has not. We say the shift is happening, it is accelerating, and early positioning matters.
- **We do not claim the MASTERY-AI Framework is complete or final.** It is version 3.1.1. It will keep evolving.
- **We do not use customer results we cannot verify.** Every case study and testimonial must be real, specific, and verifiable.

#### The Underlying Principle

Every piece of messaging should leave someone **clearer** than they were before they read it. If a message creates confusion, anxiety, or pressure instead of clarity, it is off-brand -- regardless of whether it follows every other rule in this document.

When in doubt, ask: "Would I be comfortable if the customer could see exactly how and why this message was crafted?" If the answer is no, rewrite it.
