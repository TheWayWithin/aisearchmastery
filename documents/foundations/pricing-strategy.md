## 1. Pricing Philosophy

### 1.1 Value-Based Positioning

AI Search Mastery operates two complementary SaaS products that form a continuous Diagnose-Optimize-Test-Repeat loop:

- **AImpactScanner** -- the diagnostic engine (scans websites across 27 MASTERY-AI factors)
- **LLM.txt Mastery** -- the optimization engine (generates AI-optimized llms.txt files with quality scoring)

Our pricing reflects value delivered to the customer, not cost to serve. At every tier, the customer receives measurably more value than they pay for. The pricing structure is designed so that Growth is the natural landing point for serious users of either product.

**Our Promise:** Professional-grade AI search tools at small-business prices, with a published methodology you can evaluate before you spend a penny.

### 1.2 Pricing Principles

1. **Accessibility over extraction** -- Every tier is priced for the solopreneur and small business owner, not enterprise budgets. We compete on quality and transparency, not price inflation.

2. **Value before gate** -- Free tiers deliver genuine insight. Paid tiers are earned through demonstrated value, not manufactured limitation. The product proves itself before asking for money.

3. **Transparency as trust** -- Our methodology is published. Our pricing is permanent (no promotional countdown timers). Our comparisons are honest. We acknowledge competitor strengths.

4. **Growth as gravity well** -- The entire value ladder is designed so that Growth is the most natural, most valuable, and most attractive tier for the majority of customers.

5. **Loop completeness rewards commitment** -- Higher tiers don't just give you "more." They complete the Diagnose-Optimize-Test-Repeat loop that makes the entire system work as a continuous improvement engine.

### 1.3 Cross-Product Pricing Architecture

The two products follow a consistent **2x ratio**: AImpactScanner is priced at exactly 2x LLM.txt Mastery at every paid tier. This reflects AIS's greater diagnostic depth (27 factors vs single-file optimization) while maintaining pricing coherence across the ecosystem.

At Growth and Scale tiers, AImpactScanner includes LLM.txt Mastery features **free** -- the diagnostic engine is the product at a fair price, and the optimization engine is included as a bonus. This is not a "bundle deal." It is the natural product architecture: the loop doesn't work without both tools.

## 6. Upgrade Triggers

### 6.1 Design Principles

All upgrade triggers follow these rules:

1. **Value before gate** -- User must have experienced measurable value before seeing an upgrade prompt
2. **Behaviour-based, not time-based** -- Triggers activate on usage patterns, not arbitrary calendars
3. **Explicit permission to stay** -- Every message validates the current tier
4. **Incremental framing** -- Present price as delta from current spend, not absolute
5. **Specificity over superlatives** -- Name exact features gained, never "unlock premium features"
6. **No manufactured urgency** -- No countdown timers, no "limited spots," no expiring offers
7. **Math that checks out** -- Every percentage and comparison must be mathematically verifiable

**Response:** Try the free tools. Compare the output side by side. Free tools generate identical descriptions for every page. We read each page, understand the content, and score quality on a 0-10 scale. The difference is visible in 30 seconds. The SE Ranking study showing low correlation between llms.txt and citations was measuring auto-generated files, not quality-scored ones.

**Response:** Semrush is excellent at traditional SEO. Cairrot is solid for agencies. We built everything from the ground up for AI search specifically. We move faster (no legacy code), publish our methodology (they hide theirs), and price for small business (they price for enterprise). Monthly subscription, cancel anytime, free tier to evaluate first.
