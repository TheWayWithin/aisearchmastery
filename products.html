The Tools, and What Happened to Them

This site used to sell three tools. Two of them ran as paid products and were switched off on 25 to 26 August 2026. The third was built and never opened to anyone. Nothing on this page is for sale, and there is nothing here to sign up for. What survives is the code and the framework underneath it, and both are open.

Retired, code open

AImpactScanner

A scanner that took a URL, fetched the page with a real browser so that JavaScript-rendered content was included, scored it against the MASTERY-AI framework, and returned the gaps in priority order rather than a list of everything it could measure.

It ran as a paid product and was switched off on 25 to 26 August 2026. The domain no longer resolves. There is no replacement and no waiting list.

The source is public under the MIT licence, so you can read how the scoring worked, lift the parts that are useful, or run it yourself.

github.com/TheWayWithin/aimpactscanner-mvp (opens in new tab)

Retired, code open

LLM.txt Mastery

A tool that crawled a site, worked out which pages were worth putting in front of an AI system, and wrote a spec-compliant llms.txt file from them. It also had a validator for checking a file you already had, and it handled JavaScript-rendered pages that crawl-only tools skipped.

It ran as a paid product and was switched off on 25 to 26 August 2026. The domain no longer resolves, and neither does the validator.

The source is public under the MIT licence. If you want to generate an llms.txt file today, the crawling and scoring logic in that repository is the honest starting point.

github.com/TheWayWithin/llm-txt-mastery (opens in new tab)

Never launched

AImpactMonitor

AImpactMonitor was going to watch how AI systems cited a brand over time. It was built in March 2026 and it never opened to a single user. Its infrastructure was deleted along with the other two.

This site advertised it as nearly ready for most of a year. It was not nearly ready, and saying so on the page that advertised it is the least I can do about that. There is no early access, no waiting list and no code worth pointing you at.
