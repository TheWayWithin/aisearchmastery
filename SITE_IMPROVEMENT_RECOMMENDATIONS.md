## Executive Summary

The current site is framework-heavy and technical. It speaks to *what you built* rather than *what visitors need*. The recommendations below shift focus to visitor problems, simplify messaging, and create clearer conversion paths.

**Key themes:**
1. Lead with problems, not framework features
2. Simplify — one clear offer per page
3. Build trust through results, not credentials
4. Make the next step obvious

---

## 🔴 Critical Issues

### 1. Hero Messaging is Creator-Focused, Not Visitor-Focused

**Current:**
> "Creator of the MASTERY-AI Framework v2.1 — The Industry's Most Comprehensive AI Search Optimization Methodology"

**Problem:** Visitors don't care about your framework. They care about their problem (AI isn't finding them).

**Recommendation:**
> "Your Expertise is Invisible to AI"
> 
> "ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude are answering questions in your field — but they're not mentioning you. Let's fix that."

### 2. Too Many Numbers, Not Enough Outcomes

**Current:** "132 atomic factors across 8 strategic pillars"

**Problem:** These numbers mean nothing to someone who just wants to show up in AI search results.

**Recommendation:** Replace with outcome-focused proof:
- "Clients appear in 3x more AI responses within 60 days"
- "From invisible to cited — average 47 days"
- Or simply remove the numbers entirely until you have outcome data

### 3. No Clear Single CTA

**Current:** Multiple CTAs competing (newsletter, tools, framework page, contact)

**Recommendation:** One primary CTA per page:
- **Homepage:** "Check Your AI Visibility" → leads to AImpactScanner
- **Framework page:** "Download the Framework Guide" (email capture)
- **Tools page:** Direct to tool landing pages

## 🟡 Structural Recommendations

### Homepage Structure (Recommended)

```
1. Hero
   - Problem-focused headline
   - One-sentence subhead
   - Single CTA button
   - Optional: 3-stat credibility bar (keep simple)

2. Problem Section
   - "Why AI Can't Find You" 
   - 3 clear pain points with icons
   
3. Solution Section
   - Brief intro to your approach (NOT framework details)
   - "I help [target audience] get cited by AI assistants"
   
4. Social Proof
   - Testimonials or case study snippets
   - Logos if you have them
   - "As seen in..." if applicable

5. How It Works
   - 3 simple steps
   - Step 1: Analyze → Step 2: Optimize → Step 3: Monitor
   
6. CTA Section
   - Repeat primary CTA
   - Secondary option (newsletter)

7. Footer
   - Standard links
   - Newsletter signup
```

### Remove or Relocate

- **Framework details (132 factors, 8 pillars, weights):** Move to dedicated Framework page for people who want depth
- **Personal journey/timeline:** Move to About page
- **Technical methodology comparisons:** Remove or move to blog

## 📝 Messaging Guidelines

### Words to Use More
- Visibility, found, cited, mentioned, recommended
- Clients, prospects, leads
- AI assistants, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity
- Simple, clear, systematic

### Words to Use Less
- Framework, methodology, pillars, factors
- Comprehensive, industry-leading, innovative
- Atomic, strategic, enhanced
- Version numbers

### Tone Shift
From: Academic, authoritative, technical
To: Direct, helpful, confident but approachable

---

## 🛠️ Technical Recommendations

### Performance
- Current page is 70KB+ of HTML — consider splitting
- Lazy load images below fold
- Minimize inline styles

### Mobile
- Test all CTAs are thumb-reachable
- Ensure hero text is readable without zooming
- Simplify navigation for mobile

### Analytics
- Set up goal tracking for primary CTA
- Track scroll depth on homepage
- Heat mapping if possible (Hotjar free tier)

---

## 📊 Success Metrics

After redesign, track:

1. **Bounce rate** — should decrease
2. **Time on page** — should increase slightly (not too much = content is scannable)
3. **CTA click rate** — primary metric
4. **AImpactScanner completions** — if that's your primary CTA
5. **Newsletter signups** — secondary metric
